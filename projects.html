Development projects

What counts as a development project?

In simple terms, a development project is any planned activity that changes, improves, builds or creates something in a particular location for a specific purpose, such as a factory, estate, hotel, school, hospital, filling station, shopping complex, farm, road or housing development.

How we work

A clear path from scope to approval

How an engagement typically runs. Exact steps depend on your project and the regulator.

01

Consultation & Scoping

We understand your project, location and approval needs, then agree scope, timeline and deliverables.

02

Site Visit & Baseline Data

Field teams inspect the site and collect environmental, spatial and social baseline data.

03

Assessment & Analysis

We analyse impacts and risks using field data, laboratory results and GIS analysis.

04

Mitigation & Management Plan

We design practical measures and a management plan your team can implement.

05

Reporting & Submission

We prepare clear, regulator-ready documentation and support your submission.

06

Follow-up & Monitoring

We support compliance monitoring and reporting after approval and during operations.
