Who we are

Environmental science meets geo-spatial intelligence

FIRST HABELEX GEO-SPATIAL LIMITED helps industrial companies obtain environmental approvals, meet regulatory requirements and reduce environmental compliance risks.

We are a professional environmental and geo-spatial services company committed to delivering innovative, reliable, and sustainable environmental management solutions across Nigeria. We combine environmental science, geo-spatial intelligence, field assessment, and technical compliance expertise to support government institutions, private organizations, industries, communities, and development projects.

Based in IbadanServing clients across Nigeria
